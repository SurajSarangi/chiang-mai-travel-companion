## DAY 1 — SATURDAY, DECEMBER 5
# CHIANG MAI: CULTURE, ART & CITY LIFE

**Day theme:** Discover Chiang Mai's historic side, explore its independent creative scene, eat Northern Thai food and experience the city's famous night markets.

**What we're doing**
- Exploring Chiang Mai's weekend market culture.
- Visiting historic Lanna temples in the Old City.
- Discovering Baan Kang Wat, a creative artisan village.
- Experiencing Chiang Mai Design Week.
- Ending the evening at the Saturday Walking Street and a live jazz bar.

**Places to visit**
1. Jing Jai Market — local crafts, food, produce and weekend market atmosphere.
2. Wat Phra Singh — beautiful Lanna architecture and golden temple grounds.
3. Wat Chedi Luang — impressive ancient brick chedi.
4. Wat Chiang Man — optional historic temple.
5. Baan Kang Wat — independent studios, ceramics, handmade products and creative spaces.
6. Wat Umong — forest temple and underground tunnels, optional.
7. Chiang Mai Design Week — exhibitions, creative markets and installations.
8. Wualai Road Saturday Walking Street — food, crafts and street shopping.

**Food & cafe hopping**
- Huen Muan Jai — Northern Thai lunch.
- GRAPH Cafe — specialty coffee and cold brew.
- Akha Ama Phrasing — try the dirty latte.
- Kuaytiaw 3 Baht — small noodle bowls, if we want a snack.
- Local food stalls at the Saturday Walking Street.

**Evening & nightlife**
- Saturday Walking Street.
- North Gate Jazz Co-op for live jazz and drinks.

**Highlights**
- Contrasting architecture of the Old City temples.
- A hands-on craft workshop at Baan Kang Wat, if booked.
- Chiang Mai Design Week opening events.
- Street food and local shopping at Wualai Road.
- Live jazz at North Gate.

**Suggested pace:** Medium to busy. Arrival day, so the market and optional temple stops can be shortened depending on bus arrival and energy.

## DAY 2 — SUNDAY, DECEMBER 6
# MAE KAMPONG: MOUNTAIN VILLAGE ESCAPE

**Day theme:** Leave the city behind for a day of mountain air, forest walks, wooden village houses, waterfalls and scenic coffee stops.

**What we're doing**
- Driving into the mountains east of Chiang Mai.
- Exploring a traditional village surrounded by forest.
- Walking to the waterfall.
- Enjoying a coffee with mountain and village views.
- Returning to Chiang Mai for the Sunday night market.

**Places to visit**
1. Mae Kampong Village — wooden houses, streams, mountain scenery and small local shops.
2. Mae Kampong Waterfall — forest walk and waterfall.
3. Rabiang View Mae Kampong — scenic terrace overlooking the village.
4. The Giant Chiangmai — optional alternative cafe known for its giant-tree setting.

**Food & cafe hopping**
- Breakfast or snacks in Mae Kampong.
- Local Thai/Northern Thai lunch in the village.
- Rabiang View for coffee and scenery.
- The Giant as an alternative if the treehouse cafe experience is a priority.

**Evening & nightlife**
- Sunday Walking Street on Ratchadamnoen Road.
- Street food, handmade crafts, souvenirs and local performances.

**Highlights**
- Mountain village atmosphere.
- Streamside walks and wooden houses.
- Forest waterfall.
- Scenic cafe with village views.
- Chiang Mai's famous Sunday Walking Street.

**Suggested pace:** Relaxed during the day, lively in the evening.

## DAY 3 — MONDAY, DECEMBER 7
# DOI INTHANON: THAILAND'S HIGHEST MOUNTAIN

**Day theme:** A full mountain adventure featuring a guided nature trek, cloud forest, panoramic ridge views, royal pagodas and a spectacular waterfall.

**What we're doing**
- Early-morning drive to Doi Inthanon National Park.
- Trekking the Kew Mae Pan trail with a local guide.
- Visiting the twin Royal Pagodas.
- Exploring the Ang Ka cloud forest.
- Finishing at Wachirathan Waterfall.

**Places to visit**
1. Doi Inthanon National Park — Thailand's highest mountain.
2. Kew Mae Pan Nature Trail — guided forest and mountain ridge trek.
3. Phra Mahathat Naphamethanidon and Naphaphonphumisiri — the twin Royal Pagodas.
4. Ang Ka Nature Trail — short boardwalk through a moss-covered cloud forest.
5. Wachirathan Waterfall — one of the park's most impressive waterfalls.

**Food & cafe hopping**
- Breakfast/snacks before departure.
- Lunch inside or near the national park.
- Dinner at One Nimman after returning to the city.
- Nara Thai Cuisine — Pad Thai and mango sticky rice.

**Evening & nightlife**
- Relaxed dinner at One Nimman.
- Optional stroll around the neighbourhood.
- Early night after a physically demanding excursion.

**Highlights**
- Panoramic views from Kew Mae Pan.
- Cool mountain air and possible morning mist.
- Lush cloud forest.
- Twin Royal Pagodas and landscaped gardens.
- Wachirathan Waterfall.

**Suggested pace:** Physically demanding. Early departure and a full day outdoors.

**What to carry:** Warm jacket, trekking shoes, water, sunscreen, snacks and cash for guide/park fees.

## DAY 4 — TUESDAY, DECEMBER 8
# CHIANG RAI: TEMPLES, ARCHITECTURE & ART

**Day theme:** A road trip to Chiang Rai to experience some of Northern Thailand's most unusual and visually striking architecture, including a traditional Thai costume photoshoot.

**What we're doing**
- Driving north from Chiang Mai to Chiang Rai.
- Exploring the famous White Temple.
- Wearing traditional Thai clothing for a photoshoot.
- Visiting the Blue Temple.
- Take a stop at Lallitta Cafe.
- Exploring the unconventional Black House art museum if time permits
- Returning to Chiang Mai in the evening.

**Places to visit**
1. Wat Rong Khun — White Temple, famous for its intricate white architecture and mirrored details.
2. White Temple Thai Costume Photoshoot — rent traditional Thai outfits and take photographs around the temple.
3. Wat Rong Suea Ten — Blue Temple, known for vivid blue and gold detailing.
4. Baan Dam Museum — Black House, a collection of unconventional buildings and artworks by Thai artist Thawan Duchanee.

**Food & cafe hopping**
- Breakfast before departure.
- Lunch near the White Temple or on the way to the Blue Temple.
- Coffee or refreshments during the journey, depending on timing.
- Dinner after returning to Chiang Mai.

**Evening & nightlife**
- No planned nightlife.
- Dinner near the hotel and rest.

**Highlights**
- The surreal white architecture of Wat Rong Khun.
- Traditional Thai costume experience and photoshoot.
- Vibrant blue interiors of the Blue Temple.
- Dark, unconventional artwork and architecture at the Black House.
- Experiencing three very different artistic interpretations in one day.

**Suggested pace:** Long road trip, but worth it. Private car is essential.

**What we're skipping:** Wat Huay Pla Kang / Guan Yin statue, to protect time for the costume photoshoot.
